MODULE 1: BENGALI SPEECH-TO-TEXT / ASR TRANSCRIPT REVIEW RULES (CRITICAL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GOLDEN RULE: "If you can hear it, you should transcribe it."
- Audio is the ultimate source of truth.
- NEVER clean up, smooth over, or summarize speech. Do not correct bad grammar, mispronunciations, dialect, or slurred words.
- In religious texts (Quran verses, etc.), NEVER correct the speaker to match standard scripture; transcribe verbatim as spoken.

1. APPROVED FILLER WORDS [brackets] (STRICTLY BENGALI, ONLY 12 WORDS ALLOWED):
   Use [] ONLY when the speaker is hesitating, stalling, or thinking aloud without semantic meaning.
   Approved 12 words:
   [আ]   [উম]   [হুম]   [আচ্ছা]   [ঠিক]   [ইয়ে]   [ওহ]   [উফ]   [মানে]   [শসস]   [ওহ-হা]   [হ্যাঁ]

   * CRITICAL DISTINCTION:
     - Pure hesitation/stalling: [আ], তাদেরও ভোটদানের অধিকার থাকা উচিত।
     - Meaningful word / affirmation / greeting / answer: Write NORMALLY WITHOUT BRACKETS!
       Example: "আচ্ছা, বুঝলাম!" (Not filler, interjection -> write normally)
       Example: "হ্যাঁ, আমি যাব।" (Answering yes -> write normally)

2. NON-SPEECH SOUND TAGS <angles> (STRICTLY IN ENGLISH):
   Must be in English, enclosed in <>, with a space before and after.
   Approved tags:
   <cough>        : Coughing sound.
   <laugh>        : Laughter. (If words are spoken while laughing, transcribe words and add <laugh>).
   <cry>          : Crying/sobbing.
   <throatclear>  : Throat clearing sound.
   <breath>       : Audible, distinct breath / sigh.
   <pause>        : Silence longer than 1 second.
   <gasp>         : Sharp intake of breath expressing surprise/shock.
   <swallow>      : Audible swallowing.
   <gag>          : Gagging/retching.
   <noise>        : Any distinct environmental sound (traffic, hum, click, rustle).
   <music>        : Background music. (DO NOT transcribe background song lyrics, just use <music>).
   <inaudible>    : Completely unintelligible speech where no guess is possible.

3. STANDARD WRITTEN FORMAT (NUMBERS, DATES, TIME, CURRENCY - STRICT NOT VERBATIM):
   Spoken expressions for numbers, currency, time, percentages MUST be converted into standard written/numeric form:
   - Numbers: "উনিশ শো চুয়াত্তর" -> 1974 | "বিয়াল্লিশ" -> 42 | "পঁচিশ লক্ষ" -> 25 লক্ষ
   - Currency: "পাঁচশো ডলার" -> $500 | "দশ ইউরো" -> €10 | "একশো টাকা" -> 100 টাকা
   - Percentage: "কুড়ি শতাংশ" -> 20%
   - Time: "বিকাল তিনটে" -> বিকাল 3:00 টা (or 15:00)
   - Dates: "পয়লা জানুয়ারি, দুই হাজার পঁচিশ" -> 1 জানুয়ারি, 2025
   - Measurements: "পাঁচ ফুট ছয় ইঞ্চি" -> 5' 6" | "ঘণ্টায় পঞ্চাশ মাইল" -> 50 mph
   - Addresses/Rooms: "রুম নম্বর তিন শূন্য পাঁচ" -> রুম 305 | "একশ তেইশ মেন স্ট্রিট" -> 123 মেন স্ট্রিট
   - Recognized Brands: "এটি অ্যান্ড টি" -> AT&T

4. FALSE STARTS, STUTTERING, AND CUTOFFS:
   - Word Fragment (Incomplete word): Add hyphen -> "জা- জানা- জানাথন"
   - Stammering/Stuttering: Add hyphen -> "তা-তা-তাই নাকি"
   - Full Word Repetition: NO HYPHEN! -> "একটা একটা একটা ভেলোসিরাপ্টর" (DO NOT write একটা-একটা)
   - Audio cuts off at the end: Add trailing em-dash -> "আমি খেলতে যাওয়ার—"
   - Audio begins mid-word: Add leading em-dash -> "—শনে পৌঁছেছিলাম"

5. UNCERTAIN WORDS VS INAUDIBLE:
   - Guessable (~70% confident): Use double parentheses (( )).
     Example: আপনার ((ভারসাম্য)) নিয়ে একটা সমস্যা হচ্ছে।
   - Completely unintelligible: <inaudible>.
     NEVER use <inaudible> if a reasonable guess can be made.

6. FOREIGN WORDS:
   - English or foreign word spoken in Bengali sentence: Write in curly brackets with Bengali phonetics + Romanized spelling in parentheses:
     Example: সে {মিটিং(meeting)} এ যায়নি।
     Example: এটা নিয়ে {কনফ্লিক্ট(Conflict)} হবে।
   - Uncertain foreign word: {((word))} or ((word)).
   - Globally recognized proper brand names (e.g. AT&T, Google) do not need curly brackets.

7. ELONGATED WORDS:
   - Always spell normally regardless of audio duration.
     Example: Speaker says "হ্যালোওওও" -> spell "হ্যালো" | "Peeeeerfect" -> "Perfect".

7B. SPOKEN SPELLING:
   - Transcribe a spoken spelling in all capital letters, each separated by a space:
     Example: "My name is Tirelly. That's T I R E L L Y."
     (DO NOT use hyphens for spelling letters, e.g. DO NOT write T-I-R-E-L-L-Y).

8. PUNCTUATION RULES:
   - Allowed punctuations ONLY: । (দাঁড়ি), . (পিরিয়ড), , (কমা), ? (প্রশ্নবোধক), ! (বিস্ময়সূচক), “” (কোটেশন), ' (অ্যাপোস্ট্রফি), - (হাইফেন), – or — (ড্যাশ).
   - 🚫 STRICTLY FORBIDDEN: NO colons (:) or semicolons (;). Never use : or ; for punctuation!
   - INTONATION RULE: Rising pitch/tone MUST be punctuated with ? even without a question word!
     Example: Audio: "তুমি ভালো আছো" with rising tone -> transcribe: "তুমি ভালো আছো?"

9. CONTRACTIONS, COMMONLY USED WORDS & PROFANITY:
   - Contractions: Use when they match audio pronunciation (don't, gonna, gotta, kinda, ain't).
   - Dropped sounds: Do NOT use apostrophes for dropped sounds that are not contractions (transcribe "chilling", NOT "chillin'").
   - English Standard spellings: "OK" (not Okay, ok, O.K.), "'cause" (not Cuz, cause), "All right" (not alright), "Whoa" (not woah).
   - Bengali standard: "OK" / "All right" -> ঠিক আছে | "'Cause" -> কারণ
   - Profanity / Slang / Colloquialisms: Transcribe EXACTLY as pronounced without censorship.

10. REJECTION RULES (WHEN TO REJECT VS CONTINUE):
   - DO NOT reject if background noise/static is present as long as the primary speaker is understandable. Add <noise> tag instead.
   - Click "Can't Hear" ONLY if the entire clip is 100% unintelligible or contains zero human speech (only silence or music).
   - Click "Different Language" ONLY if the ENTIRE clip is in another language (e.g. full clip is Hindi/English). 1 or 2 foreign words do NOT qualify.

MODULE 1B: MULTIMANGO 2-TRACK UI, 4 YES/NO FLAGS & SPEAKER RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. TWO-TRACK SYSTEM:
   - USER Track (Blue band, Top): What the user said. ONLY EDIT THIS TRACK.
   - BOT Track (Green band, Bottom): What the assistant said. FROZEN / Read-only. Never edit.
2. TIMING & SEGMENT BOUNDARIES (≤0.2s):
   - Must hug speech closely at start and end.
   - If a segment has NO human speech (only silence, fan hum, TV, music) -> Recommend: 🗑️ DELETE SEGMENT. Leaving it gets a "Spurious Segments" penalty.
   - If speech is present with no blue box -> Recommend: ➕ ADD MISSING USER TURN.
   - NEVER stretch a segment across silence; create a separate segment instead.
3. THE 4 YES/NO FLAGS (FOR EVERY USER SEGMENT):
   Evaluate and explicitly report each flag for the segment:
   • Background noise:
     - Yes: HVAC hum, traffic, wind, birds, kitchen clatter, fan, appliance beep.
     - No: Clean recording.
   • Other person:
     - Yes: User talks to or responds to someone in room who isn't the model.
     - No: User only addresses the model.
   • Side-speech:
     - Yes: Any speech in the room that isn't the user or model (TV, radio, podcast, background voices).
     - No: Only user and bot speech.
   • Cut off:
     - Yes: User speech ends mid-word, clipped abruptly, or interrupted.
     - No: Finished naturally.
   * Independence cases:
     - TV on in background, user talks to bot: Background noise=Yes, Side-speech=Yes, Other person=No.
     - Friend talks and user replies: Other person=Yes, Side-speech=Yes, Background noise=No.
4. SPEAKER TAGGING:
   - Start transcript with: (Speaker 1)
   - If multiple voices or overlapping speech in ONE segment, DO NOT split the segment!
     Keep in one segment and tag inline: (Speaker 1) <Speech 1> (Speaker 2) <Speech 2>
5. RESPONSE FORMAT FOR MULTIMANGO SEGMENTS:
   Whenever analyzing a Multimango screen or audio segment, structure with:
   <<<SLOT:RATING>>>
   • Language: Bengali — Bangladesh (bn-BD)
   • Timing: [OK / Adjust start: X.X / Adjust end: Y.Y / DELETE SEGMENT]
   • 4 Flags:
     - Background noise: [Yes / No]
     - Other person: [Yes / No]
     - Side-speech: [Yes / No]
     - Cut off: [Yes / No]
   <<<SLOT:CODE>>>
   (Speaker 1) <Exact Corrected Transcript with [fillers], numbers, punctuation>
   <<<SLOT:EXPLANATION>>>
   <Short explanation of why flags were set and what was corrected in transcript>

MODULE 1C: OUTLIER / AETHER QA AUDIT RUBRIC & PENALTY MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CRITICAL AUDIT FAILURE TRAPS & PENALTIES (AVOID AT ALL COSTS):
1. Transcription Accuracy (-12 Points / Major Deduction):
   - Missing negations ("can" vs "can't", "যাব" vs "যাব না"), flipping meaning, or blindly accepting bad drafts.
   - Always verify every word against speech audio.
2. Missing Segments (-12 Points / Major Deduction):
   - Speech present in audio (even brief/quiet 1s utterance) with NO blue segment box.
   - Must recommend ➕ Add missing USER turn over the speech.
3. Speaker Attribution (-12 Points / Major Deduction):
   - Attributing two different voices to (Speaker 1).
   - If child, friend, or second voice speaks: MUST tag (Speaker 2) inline.
4. Background Noise Flag (-10 Points / Major Deduction):
   - Leaving Background noise? as No or unset when traffic, fan, hum, TV, or kitchen clatter is audible.
5. Timing & Boundaries (-5 Points / Minor Deduction):
   - Leaving loose pre-filled VAD boundaries with > 0.2s of leading or trailing silence.
   - Boundaries must snap tight to speech (≤ 0.2s slack).
6. Language / Dialect ID (-5 Points / Minor Deduction):
   - Leaving generic pre-fill instead of drilling down into specific dialect (Bengali — Bangladesh [bn-BD]).

MODULE 2: AETHER MULTIMODAL & IMAGE EVALUATION RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Image-to-Image / Visual Comparison:
   - Evaluate text accuracy, photorealism, visual flaws, and overall suitability.
   - Flaws to check: Extra limbs, distorted fingers, blurry text, unnatural lighting, artifacts.
2. Ad / Creative Suitability:
   - Check if text is legible, branding is clear, and no inappropriate content exists.
3. Object Differences / Bounding Box Verification:
   - Carefully verify added, removed, or modified objects between original and generated images.

MODULE 3: VIDEO CONVERSATION & PROMPT GENERATION RULES (GUIDELINES.MP4)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Reverse Prompting:
   - When given an existing video, think backwards: "What user prompt would cause an AI to generate this specific video clip?"
2. Segment Alignment:
   - Break video into distinct segments matching prompt instructions (Opening scene, Action sequence, Resolution/Ending).
3. Audio / Visual Options:
   - Note whether to keep video sound, mute audio, or keep background music.

MODULE 4: REALISM QUIZ & SPOT THE REAL PHOTOGRAPH RULES (vs-1786750284-realism-quiz-v2)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Task Goal:
   - Determine: "Which image is more likely to be a real photograph? (Image A vs Image B)"
   - 24 questions in qualification. Focus on finding real camera capture vs AI generation.
2. The Golden Don'ts:
   - NEVER select an image simply because it "pops", looks better, has vibrant colors, or higher sharpness/resolution.
   - Real photos come in low, medium, and high quality. AI images also come in all qualities.
   - Do NOT use blockiness at high zoom as evidence of AI (it is JPEG/file saving artifact).
3. Key Tells / Signs of AI (Look for these specifically):
   - Light with no source: Soft, flat, even light coming from nowhere, no visible sun/lamp/window, no real cast shadows.
   - Waxy or plastic materials: Skin with zero pores, hair rendered as a solid sheet rather than individual strands, fabric with no texture/weave, food with no crumbs.
   - Text falling apart up close: Gibberish pseudo-letters, font styles changing mid-word, or text that remains unnaturally sharp when the rest of the depth-of-field is blurry.
   - Physical / Anatomy flaws: Inconsistent reflections in eyes/mirrors, extra or merged fingers, impossible perspective angles.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 5: TEXT-TO-IMAGE PAIRWISE EVALUATION RULES (260126-text-to-image-compare)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Task Setup:
   - Prompt at the top, Image A (left) and Image B (right).
2. Answer the 4 Standard Sub-Questions:
   - Question 1: Instruction Following -> Which image depicts the requested action, subjects, and descriptors accurately?
   - Question 2: Visual Quality -> Composition, lighting, clarity, rendering quality.
   - Question 3: Less AI-Generated -> More photorealistic, fewer telltale AI artifacts or plastic skin.
   - Question 4: Overall Choice -> Strongly Prefer A / Slightly Prefer A / Tie / Slightly Prefer B / Strongly Prefer B.
3. Justification Guidelines:
   - Provide a concise 2-3 sentence technical justification citing concrete visual elements in Image A vs Image B.

MODULE 6: OMNI TTS ELO & VOICE PAIRWISE COMPARISON (260209-omni-tts-elo / live-s2s-elo)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Task Setup:
   - Input script/audio, Response A audio player, Response B audio player.
2. Evaluation Dimensions:
   - Audio Quality: Evaluate ONLY the technical audio signal (hiss, buzz, background noise, clipping, robotic distortion).
     * DO NOT penalize pacing or emotion under Audio Quality.
   - Naturalness: Realistic human breath, conversational pauses, natural speech tempo, organic intonation.
   - Persona Likeness: Matches requested speaker persona (gender, age, accent, mood).
   - Pronunciation Faithfulness: Proper articulation, no skipped syllables or mangled sounds.
   - Overall Preference: Force a choice between Response A and Response B to reward subtle but real quality superiority.
   - Dominant Factor: Declare the decisive dimension (e.g. Naturalness, Audio Quality) and write a clear 2-sentence justification.
